## Claude Duet handoff

For the maintainer's own Claude Code sessions only. Outside contributors can skip this.

The maintainer also uses Claude Duet, an assistant in the Claude app. It can't see your
session, and you can't see it. You share a mailbox in the maintainer's private ops repo,
under `duet/` (read `duet/README.md` there once).

- **Before you start:** read `duet/outbox.md` for notes Duet left for you.
- **When you finish something or need a decision:** add a short dated note to the top of
  `duet/inbox.md`, or open an issue in this repo labeled `duet`.
- No secrets or personal details in either.
